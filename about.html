About KTU Calculator

This website helps students of APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU) calculate their Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) and Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) quickly and accurately.

What is SGPA?

Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) is the weighted average of the grade points obtained in all subjects of a semester.

What is CGPA?

CGPA is the average of all semester SGPAs earned during the entire B.Tech programme.
